## Outcome

Users can see and manage **liabilities** as first-class financial reality so that:

```text
Assets − Liabilities = Net Worth
```

is truthful — and Dashboard / Goals / Planning / AI can later reason about payoff vs saving trade-offs.

## Non-goals (this epic)

- Full loan amortization engines as a banking product
- Automatic bank scrapers for credit cards
- AI payoff recommendations (consume Debt later; do not invent a second ledger)
- Tax treatment of interest (Taxes module)
